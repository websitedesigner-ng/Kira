Kira Maison SAS ("Kira", "we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have in relation to it. Please read it carefully before using our website or purchasing from us.

1. Who We Are

Kira Maison SAS is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are registered in France under SIRET 123 456 789 00012, with our registered office at 14 Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, 75008 Paris, France.

You may contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@kira-paris.com.

2. Data We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

Category Examples Source
Identity First name, last name, title You, directly
Contact Email address, phone number, postal address You, directly
Transaction Purchase history, order reference, payment method type You, directly / payment processor
Technical IP address, browser type, device, pages visited Automatically, via cookies
Preferences Marketing opt-ins, communication preferences You, directly
Correspondence Messages sent to client relations, appointment notes You, directly

We do not collect special category data (such as health or biometric data) and we do not store full payment card numbers — these are handled exclusively by our certified payment processor.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To process and fulfil orders, including dispatch, delivery, and returns.
  • To communicate with you regarding your orders, appointments, and enquiries.
  • To manage your account and maintain your purchase history.
  • To send marketing communications where you have opted in to receive them.
  • To improve our website, services, and customer experience through analytics.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including tax, accounting, and fraud prevention.
  • To protect our legitimate business interests, including preventing fraud and ensuring website security.

4. Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Contract performance — processing necessary to fulfil an order or service you have requested.
  • Legitimate interests — processing necessary for our business operations, provided your rights are not overridden.
  • Legal obligation — processing required to comply with French and EU law.
  • Consent — where you have given clear, freely given consent, such as for marketing emails. You may withdraw consent at any time.

5. Sharing Your Data

We do not sell your personal data. We may share it with trusted third parties only where necessary:

  • Payment processors — to securely handle card transactions (e.g. Stripe).
  • Delivery partners — to fulfil and track your orders.
  • IT service providers — who host and maintain our website and systems under strict data processing agreements.
  • Professional advisors — including lawyers and accountants, where required.
  • Regulatory authorities — where we are legally required to disclose information.

All third-party processors are contractually bound to process your data only on our instructions and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. In general:

  • Order and transaction records are retained for 10 years in accordance with French commercial law.
  • Customer account data is retained for the duration of the account and up to 3 years after the last interaction.
  • Marketing consent records are retained until you withdraw consent.
  • Technical and analytics data is retained for a maximum of 13 months.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

  • Access — the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — the right to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — the right to request deletion of your data in certain circumstances.
  • Restriction — the right to request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Portability — the right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing.
  • Withdraw consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at privacy@kira-paris.com. We will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés) at cnil.fr.

8. Cookies

Our website uses cookies — small text files stored on your device — to ensure the site functions correctly and to help us understand how it is used. We use the following types:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (e.g. session management, shopping cart). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site. Used only with your consent.
  • Marketing cookies — used to deliver relevant content and track the effectiveness of campaigns. Used only with your consent.

You may manage your cookie preferences via our cookie banner or your browser settings at any time.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against accidental loss, unauthorised access, alteration, or disclosure. All data transmissions between your browser and our servers are encrypted using SSL/TLS. Access to personal data within our organisation is restricted to those with a legitimate need.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is entirely secure. If you believe your data has been compromised, please contact us immediately at privacy@kira-paris.com.

10. Children

Our website and services are not directed at persons under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy on our website with a revised date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our website following any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
