Our Responsibility

Made to Last

Longevity is the most sustainable quality a luxury object can have. We design for decades, not seasons.

At Kira, sustainability is not a campaign. It is the logical extension of what we have always believed: that a thing made well, from honest materials, for a person who will care for it, is already an act of responsibility.

Our Commitments

Six Principles We Live By

01

Longevity Over Volume

We produce fewer pieces, made better. A Kira object is built to last thirty years or more — removing it from the cycle of disposal entirely.

02

Traceable Materials

Every hide we purchase is sourced from tanneries with documented supply chains. We know the country, the process, and the standards applied to every skin.

03

Zero Waste Cutting

Our cutters work to use every usable part of the hide. Offcuts below a certain size become small leather goods. Nothing that can be used is discarded.

04

Lifetime Repair

Every Kira piece may be returned to the atelier for repair at any point in its life. We re-stitch, re-burnish, and re-finish. We do not encourage replacement.

05

Responsible Packaging

Our boxes are made from FSC-certified board. Tissue, ribbon, and inner wrapping are unbleached or plant-dyed. No plastic enters a Kira parcel.

06

Atelier Employment

All production takes place in our Paris atelier. Every artisan is a permanent employee with full benefits and a wage reviewed annually against cost of living.

I
Material Integrity

Where Our Leather Comes From

We source exclusively from European tanneries that operate under the Leather Working Group audit framework — the most rigorous environmental standard in the industry. Our primary tannery has held a Gold-rated certification since 2011.

We use full-grain vegetable-tanned leather wherever the application allows. Vegetable tanning uses plant-derived tannins rather than heavy metals, produces a richer patina with age, and is biodegradable at end of life.

Leather Working Group certified Vegetable tanning preferred 100% European sourced
II
Circular Practice

The Repair Programme

In 2019, we formalised what had always been informal practice: the Kira Lifetime Repair Programme. Any piece bearing a Kira hallmark — regardless of age or condition — may be submitted for assessment.

We repair, recondition, and restore. Where hardware is beyond repair, we replace it with current production stock, finished to match. We do not charge for assessment. We charge only material cost for repair. Our belief is that the object already paid for our time when it was first purchased.

Any age accepted No assessment fee Hardware matched & replaced
III
Atelier & People

The Hands Behind the Work

Sustainability is also a question of people. Our atelier employs forty-one artisans, all based in Paris. The average tenure is fourteen years. We do not use seasonal labour, subcontractors, or overseas production for any stage of the making.

We operate an apprenticeship programme in partnership with the Institut Français de la Mode, accepting two apprentices per year. The programme runs for three years and results in a permanent offer of employment for every apprentice who completes it.
