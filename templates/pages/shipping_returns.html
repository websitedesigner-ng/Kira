Presentation

How We Pack

The unboxing of a Kira piece is considered part of the object itself. Every order is packed by hand by a single member of the atelier team.

The Box
FSC-certified rigid board
Our signature dark brown box is made from FSC-certified rigid board, embossed with the Kira monogram in blind foil. No gloss, no varnish — only the texture of the material itself.
The Wrap
Unbleached tissue & ribbon
Each piece is wrapped in unbleached tissue paper and finished with a hand-tied grosgrain ribbon in our house colour. No plastic, no foam, no synthetic fill of any kind.
The Dustbag
Cotton flannel, hand-stamped
Every bag, wallet, and accessory comes with a natural cotton flannel dustbag, hand-stamped with the Kira mark. It is made to be kept and used.
Gifts
Complimentary gift note
All orders may include a handwritten gift note at no charge. Add your message at checkout and it will be written on Kira correspondence card and placed within the box.
Returns Policy

The Return Process

We accept returns within 14 days of delivery. Items must be unused, in their original condition, and returned in all original packaging. We do not accept returns on bespoke or personalised pieces.

01
Request a Return
Email returns@kira-paris.com with your order number and reason for return within 14 days of receiving your order. We will respond with a return authorisation number within one business day.
02
Pack the Item
Return the item in its original packaging — the Kira box, dustbag, tissue, and any accompanying cards. Items returned without original packaging may be subject to a restocking deduction.
03
Ship to the Atelier
Send the parcel to our Paris atelier using the prepaid return label we provide. We cover return shipping costs for customers in France and the EU. International customers are responsible for return shipping fees.
04
Inspection & Refund
Upon receipt, the item will be inspected by our atelier team within two business days. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5 – 10 business days, depending on your bank.
Non-returnable items: Bespoke and personalised pieces, items that have been used, altered, or show signs of wear, and items returned without original packaging are not eligible for a refund. If you are unsure about condition, please contact us before shipping.
Size or Style

Exchanges & Store Credit

We offer exchanges on eligible items within 14 days of delivery. If the item you want is not in stock, we will issue a store credit valid for 12 months.

Size Exchange
Same item, different size
Follow the standard return process and note the size you require. We will ship the replacement as soon as the original is received and inspected. Exchanges are subject to availability.
Store Credit
Valid for 12 months
If you'd prefer store credit over a refund, simply let us know. Credit is issued immediately upon return approval and can be used across any future order, including bespoke commissions.
Lifetime Care

Sending a Piece for Repair

Kira pieces may be returned to the atelier for repair at any point in their life. See our sustainability page for the full details of our repair programme. To initiate a repair, contact us with your piece's hallmark number and a description of the work required.

Assessment is always free. We charge material cost only for repair work. A full quote will be provided before any work begins. You are under no obligation to proceed.
International Orders

Duties & Customs

For orders outside the European Union, import duties, taxes, and customs fees may be levied by your country's customs authority upon arrival. These charges are the responsibility of the recipient.

Region Duties & Taxes Notes
France & EU VAT included at checkout No additional charges on delivery
United Kingdom 20% VAT + potential duty Collected by carrier upon delivery
United States Duty-free up to $800 Orders above threshold may incur duty
Canada GST/HST applicable Rates vary by province
Australia 10% GST on all imports Collected by FedEx upon delivery
Rest of World Variable Check with your local customs authority

Kira is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing. All items are declared at full purchase value in accordance with international trade law.
