Getting Started

How to Measure

Use a soft measuring tape and take measurements directly against the body — not over clothing. All dimensions below are in centimetres unless otherwise stated.

01
Waist
Wrap the tape around your natural waist — the narrowest point of your torso, typically an inch above your navel. Keep the tape level and snug but not tight.
02
Hand Circumference
Wrap the tape around the widest part of your dominant hand, just below the knuckles. Exclude the thumb. This is the measurement used for glove sizing.
03
Bag Dimensions
Bag dimensions are listed as Width × Height × Depth. Width is the widest horizontal point; height is the tallest vertical point; depth is front to back at the base.
04
Strap Length
Strap drop is measured from the top of the handle to the top of the bag body. Shoulder strap lengths are given as a range reflecting the full adjustment span.

Waist & Trouser

Belt Sizing

Belt size refers to the length from the buckle pin to the middle hole. There are always five holes, spaced 2.5 cm apart. To find your size, measure your waist at the point where you wear your belt.

Kira Size Waist (cm) Waist (inches) Total Length (cm) Middle Hole (cm)

All Kira belts are available in widths of 2 cm, 3 cm, and 4 cm. Width does not affect sizing. If your waist falls between two sizes, choose the larger.
