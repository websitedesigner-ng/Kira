Where It Began

Kira was founded in a quiet atelier on the Rue du Faubourg Saint-Honoré, where a single craftsman set out to make objects of enduring beauty. Not fashion — objects. Pieces that would carry the weight of time without flinching.

From the beginning, the philosophy was simple: use only the finest materials, work only with hands that understand patience, and never compromise the integrity of the object for the speed of the season.

The Hands

A Devotion to Craft

Every Kira piece passes through a minimum of twelve pairs of hands before it is considered complete. Our master artisans — many of whom have spent decades perfecting a single technique — work without shortcuts.

The leather is selected by touch before it is selected by sight. The stitching is counted, not measured. The finish is applied in silence, because silence is the only condition under which true attention is possible.

✦
La Philosophie

Deliberate Scarcity

In an age of abundance, Kira is a study in restraint. We produce fewer pieces than we could. We decline more clients than we accept. We have never held a sale, and we never will.

This is not arrogance. It is a form of respect — for the object, for the craftsman who made it, and for the person who will carry it. A Kira piece is not a purchase. It is a relationship.

The Maison Today

Rooted in Tradition

More than a century after that first atelier, Kira remains a family house. The same families who stitched the first bags still send their children to learn the craft. The same suppliers who provided the first hides still tan our leather.

The world has changed enormously. Kira has changed only in the ways that matter — learning, refining, deepening — while holding fast to the things that do not need to change at all.
